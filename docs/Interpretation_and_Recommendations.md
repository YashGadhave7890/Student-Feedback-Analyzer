# Interpretation and Recommendations
### Student Feedback Intelligence System

**Dataset:** 130 student course evaluations  
**Methods:** Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) Topic Modeling + Supervised Multi-Class Sentiment Analysis  

---

## 1. Summary of Discovered Topics

Using unsupervised LDA ($K=5$), the feedback naturally groups into five distinct themes:

1. **Practical Labs and Hands-on Learning (46.9%)**:
   - The majority of comments focus on lab assignments, real-world tools (Docker, Power BI, Python, R), and hands-on exercises.
   - **Key takeaway:** This is the flagship strength of the curriculum and should be preserved and expanded.

2. **Learning Resources and Materials (33.1%)**:
   - Comments discuss slides, lecture notes, supplementary reading, and online materials.
   - **Key takeaway:** Students expressed concerns about slide length and overwhelming reading materials before exams.

3. **Assessment Methods and Feedback (10.0%)**:
   - Focuses on quizzes, CATs, assignments, and timeliness of grading.

4. **Course Content and Structure (8.5%)**:
   - Comments on logical sequencing, pacing, and bridging theory with practicals.

5. **Teaching Quality and Engagement (1.5%)**:
   - Reviews discussing lecture delivery and instructor engagement.

---

## 2. Sentiment Breakdown & Key Recommendations

- **Positive (27%)**: High appreciation for practical lab sessions, interactive problem solving, and tool exposure.
- **Neutral (56%)**: Constructive feedback acknowledging course value while suggesting adjustments in pacing and instructions.
- **Negative (17%)**: Concentrated around confusing lab step-by-step instructions and long lecture slide decks.

### Recommendations for Instructors:
1. **Streamline Lecture Slides:** Condense high-density slide decks into modular executive summaries for exam revision.
2. **Standardize Lab Documentation:** Provide step-by-step prerequisites and troubleshooting guides at the start of complex API / Docker labs.
3. **Pacing Balance:** Allocate additional lab hours for advanced topics to maintain high engagement without student fatigue.
